# GOAI UI / UX full redraw prompt

Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop web application redesign reference
Primary request: Completely redraw the GOAI options research terminal as a production-grade professional research workstation. This is a visual direction reference for rebuilding the real HTML/CSS/JS interface, not a marketing landing page.
Audience: professional equity and options researchers who need to compare evidence, cost, risk and scenario assumptions quickly.
Scene/backdrop: a quiet editorial market-research workstation, structured like a printed analyst's desk with a precise app shell and visible information hierarchy.
Subject: GOAI research terminal for Tencent 0700.HK before earnings. Show a left module navigator, a wide central research canvas, and a right decision inspector. The page must feel like a serious instrument designed by a product team, not an AI-generated dashboard.
Style/medium: high-fidelity UI mockup, editorial industrial software, crisp typography, disciplined grid, restrained borders, tactile paper and graphite surfaces, no decorative 3D objects.
Composition/framing: 16:9 desktop viewport, three-column shell. Left navigation is a real vertical module list. Center has one dominant conclusion statement, then two evidence columns and a compact price/risk table. Right inspector has current conclusion, risk budget, expiry, and a small analytical visual. Keep generous but intentional whitespace and strong asymmetry.
Color palette: charcoal blue-black navigation, warm off-white canvas, muted steel blue, one vermilion warning accent, one restrained sea-green positive accent. No purple, no neon glow, no gradient mesh.
Materials/textures: faint paper grain only, hairline rules, monospaced numerals for prices and timestamps, editorial grotesk headings, no glassmorphism.
Text (verbatim, limited and legible): "GOAI", "研究台", "总览", "决策卡", "期权链", "宏观", "投研", "政策库", "辩论", "这次先不交易", "机会不足", "风险可控", "主方案", "风险预算". Do not display internal enum codes.
Constraints: make the entire UI feel intentionally designed and usable at a glance; show functional navigation and clear hierarchy; use rectangular sections and thin rules instead of a pile of rounded cards; keep components visually coherent; the right rail must be useful, not decorative.
Avoid: generic SaaS dashboard, AI assistant aesthetic, chatbot bubbles, floating gradient blobs, purple-blue neon, excessive rounded cards, pill badges everywhere, glowing borders, sparkles, robots, stock-photo people, fake charts with illegible labels, dense unreadable microtext, visible strings such as NO_TRADE, LOW_EDGE, Edge Gate, Risk Gate, Action Gate.
